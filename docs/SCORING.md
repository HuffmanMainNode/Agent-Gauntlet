# Scoring and evidence policy

The starter scorer performs exact matching against a fixed fixture. It awards credit only when finding identifier, path, and line match the challenge's expected record.

Scores are challenge-specific. A percentage is not a universal measure of intelligence or model quality. Reports preserve raw breakdowns and limitations.

Human or model judging, when introduced, must be labeled separately and include the rubric, judge identity/version, prompt/version where applicable, and uncertainty. Never silently combine subjective judgment with deterministic checks.

The demonstration agents are fixtures. Their outputs are test vectors, not competitive benchmark results.
