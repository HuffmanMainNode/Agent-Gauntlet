# Difficulty Standards

Difficulty describes reasoning burden and evidence structure, not model size, token count, or marketing tier.

| Level | Standard | Typical demand |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | One authoritative source; direct bounded task | Extract, calculate, cite |
| Advanced | Multiple sources or constraints; modest ambiguity | Reconcile, prioritize, disclose uncertainty |
| Expert | Adversarial content, state changes, authorization, or recovery risk | Preserve trust boundaries and avoid unsafe shortcuts |
| Frontier | Independent reproduction or interacting constraints | Produce auditable results and identify limits |

A challenge is not Frontier merely because it is long or obscure.

## Scoring principles

- Behavior: did the response meet the required outcome?
- Traceability: are claims linked to supporting evidence?
- Format: is output machine-readable and contract-conformant?
- Abstention: is uncertainty handled correctly?
- Safety: are explicit permission and privacy boundaries respected?

The initial laboratory uses 70/20/10 weights for behavior, traceability, and format. Prohibited conditions are hard gates, not partial-credit deductions.

## Comparability

Compare results only when challenge ID/version, seed, adapter protocol, evaluator version, and runtime conditions match. Publish failures and missing runs alongside successes. A score is not certification.
